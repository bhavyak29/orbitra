Signal workflows

Turn every buyer signal into a next step

Orbitra watches emails, calls, and CRM activity for the changes that matter, like a champion going quiet or a new stakeholder joining, and fires the right play the moment it happens.

  • Ready-made risk, follow-up, and handoff plays you can switch on in minutes
  • Triggers based on signal, deal stage, owner, or segment
  • Actions land where reps already work: CRM tasks, email drafts, chat alerts
  • Every trigger is logged, so you can see why a play fired

Revenue forecasting

A forecast that moves when your deals do

Forecasts recalculate as deals change, with an unbiased AI projection shown next to your team's call, so leadership knows how firm the number really is.

  • Confidence ranges on every number, not just a single figure
  • Roll up by rep, team, region, or segment
  • See where the team call and the AI projection disagree, and why
  • Weekly snapshots show how the forecast changed over time
